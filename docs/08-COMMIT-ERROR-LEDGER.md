## How this list is kept

A commit that admits an open item is not a mistake — pretending it does not exist is. Each row above
was verified against the current tree rather than taken from the message: a row is only **closed**
when a file or a test in `main` proves it. When the bare-world chunk lands, its row moves to
**closed** and names the test that does it.
| `c2897fe` — The gallery shows the session that now closes with a green drill | **Closed by `406e00e` (the ignore rules were lost and the media walked back in).** `tools/demo-pack.mjs` was re-cut to rebuild the gallery, which put `demo/screens` (13 MB) and `demo/video` (25 MB) on disk; the ignore rules that had kept them out were no longer in `.gitignore` at all, so `git add demo` staged the lot and 38 MB of screenshots and recordings entered the commit that was meant to carry one 1.8 MB page. | **closed** | The rules are written at the **end** of `.gitignore` — `demo/screens/`, `demo/video/` — where nothing above them can silently unignore the directories, the files are out of the index and off the disk, and the two commits that only existed to undo the slip were squashed into one. The last two commits were rewritten (`filter-branch` index-filter, original ref deleted, reflog expired, `gc --prune=now`) and force-pushed **with a lease**; `size-pack` is 10.03 MiB and `git log --all -- demo/screens demo/video` is empty. The check that would have caught it is now in the ledger's own terms: after `git add`, `git ls-files demo/screens demo/video | wc -l` must be 0. |
| `815b187` — The store's write path, first pass | **Closed by the store itself on the day it was written: two filings, one identity.** The outbox called a record by its register and its id — `filing|SUB-000001:filed` — and the conventional and the takaful returns *both* number from `SUB-000001`. The chain happily held two entries under one name, so a reader keying by that name could not tell the two apart, and the store refused the second as a duplicate of the first — which is how the defect announced itself, on a real payload (`item 18 of 18 ... appears twice in this batch`).
